Privacy policy

Personal Hub is a personal project with a single user: its developer, Summer Deehan. It isn't offered to the public and doesn't collect data from, or about, anyone else.

Google Calendar access. The project requests read-only access to the developer's own Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), used solely to include that day's events in a daily briefing message sent to the developer. Calendar data is read at most once a day, is never stored beyond the current briefing, and is never shared with, sold to, or used by any other person or service.

Other data. The project also keeps a personal to-do list and notes, stored privately and used only by the developer.
